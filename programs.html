Our work

Nine sectors. One community-centered approach.

Over the next three years, AHNO will continue to focus its operations on priority humanitarian and early recovery sectors — implementing integrated, multi-sectoral interventions aligned with humanitarian principles and national and international response priorities.

Priority programmatic areas

Where we put our effort.

01

Humanitarian Assistance

Delivery of life-saving emergency assistance, including multi-purpose cash, in-kind support, and emergency response to conflict, disasters, and displacement-affected populations.

02

Health Services

Support to primary health services, community health outreach, referral support, and emergency health interventions in underserved and crisis-affected areas.

03

Public Nutrition Services

Nutrition interventions focused on preventing and treating malnutrition among children under five, pregnant and lactating women, and other vulnerable groups.

04

Educational Services

Support for access to safe, inclusive, and quality education through community-based education, learning support, and education-in-emergencies programming.

05

Agriculture & Irrigation

Strengthening agricultural productivity through inputs, irrigation rehabilitation, climate-resilient farming practices, and technical capacity building for farmers.

06

Food Security & Livelihoods

Supporting household food access and resilience through livelihood restoration, income generation activities, market-based interventions, and vocational skills.

07

Social Services

Community-based social support targeting vulnerable groups, including persons with disabilities, women-headed households, returnees, and marginalized populations.

08

WASH & Shelter

Access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene promotion, plus emergency and transitional shelter support and shelter management.

09

Third-Party Monitoring, Research & Evaluation

Independent monitoring, assessments, research studies, and evaluation services supporting evidence-based programming and accountability to affected populations and donors.

AHNO implements both small-scale and large-scale projects across these sectors to ensure access to basic services, strengthen community resilience, and support the survival and dignity of vulnerable populations at the community level.

Geographical presence

Operational capacity across eleven provinces.

AHNO maintains strong operational capacity and implementation experience across multiple provinces of Afghanistan, enabling effective and timely delivery of humanitarian and development interventions. The organization currently operates in, but is not limited to, the following provinces:

Kabul — Head Office Ghazni Parwan Farah Bamyan Wardak Khost Kandahar Helmand Balkh Herat

Through its established field networks, trained technical staff, and community-based structures, AHNO ensures efficient program implementation, monitoring, and coordination at the provincial and district levels.

AHNO also maintains surge operational capacity, allowing the organization to rapidly scale up and expand operations to additional provinces in response to emerging humanitarian needs, sudden-onset emergencies, and partner or donor requirements — enabling timely, needs-based humanitarian response across Afghanistan.

Scaling where the need is greatest.

Surge capacity and established field networks mean AHNO can move quickly when a new crisis emerges.
