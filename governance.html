Governance

Structured oversight. Accountable delivery.

AHNO operates under a structured governance framework that ensures strategic direction, transparency, accountability, and effective operational management.

How AHNO is organized.

The Board of Directors provides strategic oversight, policy approval, risk management, and compliance assurance. Day-to-day operations are led by the General Director, supported by senior management and technical teams across programs, operations, finance, MEAL, and support functions.

Board of Directors

Strategic leadership and oversight.

The Board of Directors provides strategic leadership and institutional oversight to ensure that AHNO operates in line with its mission, vision, and humanitarian principles, ensuring strong governance, accountability, and transparency across all operations.

Key roles and responsibilities

  • Providing strategic direction and long-term organizational vision
  • Approving organizational policies, strategies, and annual plans
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory, and donor requirements
  • Overseeing financial management and fiduciary responsibilities
  • Supporting resource mobilization and external representation when required
  • Ensuring organizational accountability and risk oversight

Organizational policies & systems

Comprehensive internal governance.

AHNO has developed internal systems and policies fully aligned with international humanitarian standards, national regulations, and donor requirements — regularly reviewed to stay aligned with global best practice, emerging humanitarian standards, and risk management principles.

Financial Management & Internal Controls

Budgeting, accounting, internal audits, and financial reporting that ensure accountability and transparency in the use of resources.

Administration Policy

Clear guidance on organizational operations, office management, documentation, and administrative responsibilities.

Procurement & Logistics Procedures

Standardized, fair and competitive procurement and supply chain processes, with proper asset management.

Human Resources Policy & Code of Conduct

Recruitment, performance management, staff development, ethical behavior, and workplace conduct standards.

Safeguarding & PSEA

Strict adherence to safeguarding policies, survivor-centered approaches, staff awareness, and reporting mechanisms.

Anti-Fraud & Anti-Corruption Policy

Zero tolerance for fraud, corruption, bribery, and misuse of resources, with clear reporting and investigation procedures.

Risk Management Policy

Identifies, assesses, and mitigates operational, programmatic, and financial risks to ensure resilience and continuity.

Data Protection & Confidentiality

Secure collection, storage, and management of sensitive beneficiary, partner, and staff information.

Communication Policy

Guidance on internal and external communications, media engagement, donor reporting, and branding.

Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)

Communities at the center.

AHNO is committed to placing affected populations at the center of all its humanitarian and development interventions — engaging communities in program design, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation.

Community Consultations & Participatory Assessments

Community members, including women, youth, and vulnerable groups, are actively involved in needs assessments, program planning, and decision-making.

Feedback & Complaints Response Mechanisms (CFRM)

Structured channels enable beneficiaries to give feedback and raise concerns safely and confidentially — all documented, analyzed, and acted on.

Dedicated Community Focal Points

Trained focal points serve as a direct link between field teams and affected populations, supporting grievance resolution.

Adaptation Based on Beneficiary Feedback

Programs are regularly reviewed and adjusted based on community input and monitoring data to stay relevant and responsive.

Gender, Protection & Inclusion (GPI)

Safe, dignified, and accessible to all.

AHNO mainstreams gender equality, protection, and inclusion across every program.

Women's Participation & Leadership

Opportunities for participation, decision-making, skills development, and leadership at community and project levels.

Safeguarding Children & Vulnerable Adults

Child protection measures and safeguards that ensure rights, safety, and access to services.

Including Persons with Disabilities

Interventions designed for full access and participation in programs and decision-making.

Conflict-Sensitive, Do-No-Harm

Careful consideration of local context and conflict dynamics to minimize harm and reduce risk.
