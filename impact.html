Impact & partners

A proven track record, delivered with national partners.

AHNO has extensive experience implementing humanitarian assistance, assessments, monitoring, and research activities across Afghanistan — delivering projects funded by both national and international partners.

Key areas of implementation

What we've delivered.

Emergency Response & Food Assistance

Timely delivery of life-saving food and non-food items to populations affected by conflict, disasters, and displacement.

Health & Nutrition Assessments

Comprehensive assessments informing program design, monitoring malnutrition trends among children and at-risk groups.

Market Assessments & Baseline/Endline Surveys

Evidence-based data collection supporting livelihoods, market-based interventions, and food security programming.

Third-Party Monitoring (TPM)

Independent verification of food and cash interventions, ensuring transparency and adherence to donor standards.

Women-Led Business Research

Studies and assessments strengthening women's economic participation and gender-sensitive livelihood programs.

Value proposition

Why partner with AHNO.

AHNO offers unique advantages to donors, partners, and stakeholders seeking effective, accountable, and context-responsive programming in Afghanistan.

1

Strong local access & community acceptance

Long-standing presence and trusted relationships with communities, local authorities, and stakeholders enable timely, culturally appropriate interventions — even in hard-to-reach or conflict-affected areas.

2

Proven community-based delivery

A track record of designing and delivering programs that actively engage communities, build local capacities, and ensure participation, ownership, and sustainability.

3

Proven TPM & assessment capacity

Extensive experience in third-party monitoring, baseline and endline surveys, and needs assessments — ensuring evidence-based design and accountability.

4

Cost-effective & flexible implementation

High-quality programs delivered with efficient use of resources and the ability to adapt rapidly to evolving contexts and donor priorities.

5

Experienced national technical staff

A multidisciplinary team of qualified Afghan professionals across humanitarian, development, MEAL, gender, protection, livelihoods, agriculture, health, and education.

6

Rapid deployment & surge capacity

AHNO can quickly scale up operations and mobilize teams in response to sudden-onset emergencies across multiple provinces.

A trusted implementing partner.

Gain a capable, flexible partner with proven capacity to deliver results while maintaining strong accountability, inclusivity, and protection standards.
